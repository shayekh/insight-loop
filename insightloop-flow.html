Everything sits under a business unit that already exists — business unit creation isn't part of this flow. Business units hold accounts (clients, e.g. Service 7000), accounts hold projects (engagements, e.g. Customer Portal), and each project runs any number of quarters against one tagged template.

Entity hierarchy

Business unit is pre-seeded; template is the one entity that isn't strictly owned by a project.

Scope isn't picked directly — it falls out of how far you tag down the chain. Leave everything blank and it's Global; add a business unit, then an account, then a project to narrow it step by step.

Global
Nothing tagged below — usable on any project, in any business unit.
◉ Nothing set → Global (the case right now). Add a business unit → narrows to Business Unit. + account → narrows to Account. + project → narrows to Project. Account only unlocks once a business unit is set, and project only once an account is set. Both are picked from what already exists — they can't be created here, so the account/project must exist before you can tag a template to it.
